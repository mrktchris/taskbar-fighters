## V3.1 (2026-10-13): "on top of each other and not doing anything" - fixed and seen on the live taskbar

The author's report: slot a (Claude, plate `/reverse working ctx 74%`) and slot b (Gemini, idle) stood in the same spot and did nothing.

### Root causes (live evidence, read-only first)

- **Overlap.** Bus showed a at x 616 then 557 (its half is the LEFT one, 52-446 then) with b wandering 494-888 (passed 567, 9 px away); the
  real window rects agreed (`minis-a` 525-704, `minis-b` 534-713). a had been knocked out (`limit`, ctx 92.5%) during a handoff in the
  middle. Three brain rules kept it there: a KO'd fighter "stops where it stands"; `state == limit and mode == walk` aborted *every* walk,
  including the walk home; and nothing ever sent a non-idle fighter back to its half (only idle wandering moved it). `set_paused` also
  overwrote `home_x` with wherever the fighter was, and a drag could put a fighter in the other half.
- **No action.** Bouts needed *both* working (clash) or *both* idle (spar); Claude working + Gemini idle matched neither, so no bout could
  ever start. After the one console on the limit event, a KO'd partner was never visited again. Working = a subtle typing loop.

### Fixes

- Brain: a fighter outside its own half walks back (after KO / get-up); a limit fighter may walk home; drag clamps to its half; pause no
  longer moves home. Any awake pair interacts: clash when either works, spar/ranged when neither does (45-90 s); repeat console visits
  (40-80 s); working fighters shadowbox (a strike every 3-6 s). Bouts are staged in the middle of the fighting zone.
- Plates: line 1 is the program **name** (atlas `display`; program name for the generic skin). Both plates are separate fixed windows at the
  left end of the zone; fighters live in `fight_zone` (x 340-900), so a sprite (max 33 px from the hip) can never reach a plate (end x 300).
  `minis.py plates on|off` and a menu item, persisted in `control.json`.
- `minis.py demo [spar|clash|ranged]` and menu *Start a bout now*: forces only the choreography (accepted whatever the states), not the plates.
- Slot logs record bout / contact / hit times (ids, times, move names only). Mutex names are per data dir (so the test can run beside live fighters).

## V5 (2026-10-13): fight choreography and beam playback - engine half

Written by Claude (engine half: `minis.py`, `minis_core/**`, `minis_win/**`, tests except `test_art.py`, `tools/**`); the six character
redesigns and the beam FX overhaul (`art/`, `assets*/`, `tests/test_art.py`) were built in parallel by the ART agent, and the engine reads
every frame count, `fx` list, `grow` and `flash` key from the JSON at run time.  The author's verdict on V4, watching live: "same moves over
and over and that beam looks terrible".  Verified causes: `rounds.py` / `brain.py` drew every attack with `rng.choice` from the six strikes
(the first spar exchanges from `pool[:4]`), nothing stopped repeats, and defence / locomotion / specials / supers / the ultimate almost never
appeared; Claude's beam ultimate played as one static frame set with no extension or travel.

### Built

- `minis_core/choreo.py` (new): a seeded no-repeat `Picker` (never the same move twice in a row, no move more than twice a round,
  weighted by category and situation) and `plan_exchange`, the exchange grammar: openers (dash-in jab, jump-in kick, sweep), defender
  answers (block / block_hit, crouch_block under highs, parry -> counter, backdash whiff, getting hit by power), jab-cross-uppercut
  combos with hitstop, pressure vs a spacing reset (walk_back / backdash), specials at range, a super when the hit meter fills, the
  ultimate once per match at a dramatic moment (round point / low HP, never in a round's first two exchanges) with flash + shake,
  knockdown -> getup on big hits, a taunt after a whiffed ultimate, one air-to-air exchange per round.  Pure function of one `Random`.
- `rounds.py`: `Bout` plays those beats into a lazy-idle `_Emitter` with positions (`to` slides: dash-ins, backdashes, knockback),
  `hitstop`, hit meters, projectiles, `@shake` / `@flash` / `@whiff` directives, impact bursts, rollback at the 25 s cap.  `make_match`
  and the new `make_friendly` (spar / clash) share it; `brain.make_timeline` only adds the bows and the fist bump.  Both processes still
  derive the identical timeline from the plan.
- `brain.py`: hitstop freeze of the animation clock, slides, the three new directives, `flash` in the view.
- `fx.py` / `effects.py`: `grow {from_w, to_w, ms}` (extends from the hand, holds, retracts + fades over the last frame; `extent()`,
  cropped placement in both facings, `beam_end()`, `reach()`), `flash: true` (one-frame white, ~60 %), standalone move shakes and flashes.
  Absent keys fall back to the V4 behaviour.  `render.compose` whitens only the fighter's drawn pixels on the taskbar (plate-safe).
- `arena.py`: exchanges are the same grammar played with physics (approach / beat / strike states, resets with velocity, counters swap
  roles), hitstop freezes both bodies, hover for air-to-air, beam ultimates fired from `reach * 0.8` so the beam reaches the hurtbox
  (travelling ones from 260 px, contact at arrival), impact burst at the beam's end + `<move>_impact` / explosion + flash + the move's
  own shake, knockback straight along a beam, knockdown -> getup when HP is left, a ceiling under the HUD, window tops inside the HUD
  band are crack targets but not platforms, `ko_stars` fade before the next round, a full-frame flash in the compositor, and the
  heartbeat carries the last 16 content-free events (kind, time, effect name).
- `minis.py arena --seed K`, `tools/arena_capture.py` (PrintWindow of the arena's own window at the beam / flash / air / knockdown
  moments, driven by the heartbeat), `tools/make_previews.py --match-gif <path>`, `gen_movelist.py` scans `choreo.py` (3 moves left
  untriggered: turn, crouch, facepalm; was 10).

## Windows on strangers' PCs (2026-10-21, branch `win-robust`, not merged)

Goal (owner: "apply all"): run on other people's Windows PCs, not only the author's 2560x1440, 100%, bottom-taskbar setup.
Claude wrote it; an independent review is owed like every Claude-written pass.

### Built

- **`config.stage()`** (pure, shared geometry, tested on every OS): from the backend's rect-only `info` (taskbar rect, edge,
  auto-hide + revealed, monitor, work area, DPI, element rects) -> edge, layout (`taskbar` band or bottom-of-work-area
  `strip`), usable / why, integer scale, band, floor, zone (logical px) and where the zone came from. Helpers:
  `occupied_spans` (leaf rects, plus containers their children fill; the whole bar and Windows 10's free-spanning task list
  do not count), `free_gaps`, `pick_gap` (the leftmost wide gap wins at 3/4 of the widest: left of centred icons, right of
  left-aligned ones), `zone_from_span` (40 px from a monitor edge, 8 px + the scoreboard's 36 px tail from an element,
  element-bounded ends on a 48 px grid so independently-detecting processes agree and not every icon re-lays out),
  `autohide_shown`, `edge_of`, `band_for_dpi`, `apply_stage`. A `zone` in `minis.json` turns `zone_auto` off and wins.
- **`minis_win/shell.py`**: DPI awareness per-monitor v2 -> v1 -> shcore -> system, and `dpi_awareness()` reports what the
  process really has; `taskbar_info(monitor)` (primary `Shell_TrayWnd`, or with `monitor` that monitor's
  `Shell_SecondaryTrayWnd`); `monitor_dpi`; `monitors()` gains each monitor's scale; element rects from
  **`minis_win/uia.py`** (raw-COM UI Automation via ctypes, reads only BoundingRectangle + IsOffscreen, on a worker thread
  with a 1.5 s deadline so a hung Explorer cannot freeze a loop) with the shell's own `Start` / `TrayButton` /
  `TrayNotifyWnd` / (Windows 11) `MSTaskListWClass` window rects as the fallback, cached 5 s; `layout_epoch()` bumped by
  WM_DISPLAYCHANGE, WM_DPICHANGED, WM_SETTINGCHANGE(SPI_SETWORKAREA) and TaskbarCreated.
- **`minis.py`**: `taskbar_stage()` used by the fighter, the scoreboard, `status` and `doctor`; a shell without
  `taskbar_info` (macOS, test fakes) is laid out exactly as before. Fighters re-zone live (`Fighter.rezone`: new fight zone and
  homes, a running bout called off, plates follow); the scoreboard follows the zone; the select popup opens above the band on
  its monitor; an auto-hide bar is polled every 0.25 s; a layout-epoch change re-lays out at once.
- **Tray**: the icon is reloaded at the new small-icon size on WM_DPICHANGED / WM_DISPLAYCHANGE.
- `doctor` prints `dpi`, `stage` and `zone` lines; README (Configuration, where the fighters stand, Known limits), AGENTS.md
  (preflight and troubleshooting), CHANGELOG (Unreleased).

Decisions: vertical and top taskbars get a horizontal strip at the bottom of the work area, not a vertical lane (the art,
the choreography and the bout planner are horizontal; a vertical lane would mean a second engine). Sprite and HUD share one
integer scale, the largest whose 44 px scoreboard fits the band (`ui_scale`): 1x at 100-175%, 2x at 200%. Sharp pixels and a
HUD that never spills out of the taskbar beat matching the 100% size at 150%.
